## Get your token

1. Sign in to the **OVHcloud control panel**.
2. Open **Public Cloud** and select your project.
3. Left menu: **AI & Machine Learning > AI Endpoints**.
4. Open the **API keys** tab and create a key.
5. Copy the key. You will paste it in the next step.

The same key works for every model exposed by AI Endpoints.
Anonymous access (no key) is limited to 2 requests per minute and is not supported by this extension.
